## Quarter 2 Project Brainstorming

**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic mentioned in our domain is fractal chain of thought. Fractal chain of thought is a method to prompt AIs and LLMs to provide a more complex depth understanding of topics and tasks. Essentially, you would prompt by including a clear detailed task, provide rubrics and functions for the AI to use to do the task according to how you specify. Afterwards, you also specify to the AI to do three iterations of analysis going from macro to meso to micro levels. This overall makes the task more detailed, allowing for higher accuracy and better results. I found this interesting because it allowed me to underestand the full potential of AI and how the human plays an important role in steering AI. A human is the one to help establish the prompts, making the rubrics and functions an AI must follow and ultimately is the one reviewing the outputs from AI. This is interesting because it keeps me in check of how I am supposed to contribute to work and verify the outputs of AI.
